I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the [HKUST NLP Group](https://github.com/hkust-nlp) and am supervised by [Prof. Junxian He](https://github.com/HKUST-NLP). Before joining HKUST, I completed my undergraduate studies at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), graduating in June 2024, and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning, covering LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Bio

I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science at HKUST, supervised by Prof. Junxian He. I graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024 with a BEng and received the Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship during my undergraduate studies. Since starting my PhD I have carried out research internships at MINIMAX (2025 - present), Tencent WXG (2024), and Shanghai AI Lab (2023).

## Research interests
