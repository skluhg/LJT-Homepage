About
======
I am **Junteng Liu**, a first-year PhD candidate in the Department of Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), and a member of the HKUST NLP Group, where I work with my supervisor Professor Junxian He. I received my B.Eng. from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024.

My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning. I am interested in **LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning**, **hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs)**, and **LLM truthfulness and interpretability**.
