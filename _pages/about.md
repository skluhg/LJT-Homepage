About
======
I am a first-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), where I am a member of the HKUST NLP Group and am supervised by Professor Junxian He. I completed my B.Eng. at Shanghai Jiao Tong University in June 2024. My research lies at the intersection of natural language processing and machine learning.

Academic background
======

  * Graduated in June 2024
  * Zhiyuan Honor Scholarship
